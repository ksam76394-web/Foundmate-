# Foundmate-
FoundMate is a smart lost-and-found platform that bridges the gap between lost belongings and their owners. It eliminates the hassle of searching endlessly or relying on word of mouth by allowing users to report, discover, and reconnect with misplaced items through one simple, organised system.

Key Objectives:

1. To provide a simple and organised platform for reporting lost and found items.
2. To reduce the time and effort involved in searching for misplaced belongings.
3. To connect people who have lost items with those who have found them.
4. To enable users to search for items using relevant details such as item name, date, and location.
5. To minimise dependency on word of mouth and manual searching.
6. To promote a responsible and reliable community-based approach towards returning lost belongings.

## Working of the Project – FoundMate

FoundMate works as a centralised lost-and-found platform that allows users to report, search, and recover misplaced belongings through a simple and organised process.

### Step-by-Step Working

1\. User Registration/Login: Users access the platform and enter their basic details to use its features.

2\. Report a Lost Item: If a user loses something, they can submit a report by entering the item name, description, date, and location where it was lost.

3\. Report a Found Item: Users who find someone else's belongings can register the item on the platform by providing its details and the location where it was found.

4\. Search and Discover: Users can browse or search for reported items using relevant information such as item name, date, and location.

5\. Matching Information: The platform helps users identify potentially matching lost and found items by comparing their details.

6\. Contact and Recovery: When a possible match is identified, users can connect and coordinate to verify ownership and safely recover the item.

7\. Update Item Status: Once the belonging is successfully returned, its status can be updated to indicate that the item has been recovered.
